Passage 2

Many students believe that intelligence alone determines academic success. However, research and everyday experience suggest that consistent effort is also extremely important. A student who studies regularly may achieve better results than a naturally talented student who does not practice. Effective learning also requires students to understand their mistakes. Instead of becoming discouraged after failing a test, successful learners analyze their errors and identify areas that need improvement. This process allows them to develop better strategies for future challenges. In conclusion, academic success is usually the result of several factors, including effort, effective learning strategies, and persistence. Talent can be helpful, but it cannot replace regular practice and determination.
